Late Payment Policy

A payment is considered late when it is not received by the contractual due date.

For mortgage accounts, a late fee may be assessed after the applicable grace period.

Accounts that remain unpaid may become delinquent and may be subject to additional servicing actions.

Customers experiencing payment difficulty should be offered available assistance options or escalation to a servicing specialist.

FinAgent must not promise fee waivers, interest-rate changes, or delinquency removal unless an authorized servicing workflow confirms eligibility.
